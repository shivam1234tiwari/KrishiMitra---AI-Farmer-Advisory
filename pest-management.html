Common Pests & Diseases by Crop

Rice - Common Pests & Diseases

High Risk Period: July-October

Yellow Stem Borer

Insect Pest
High Damage
Symptoms
  • Dead hearts in vegetative stage
  • White ear heads at maturity
  • Presence of caterpillars inside stems
  • Yellowing and drying of leaves
Control Measures
Cultural
  • Remove and destroy stubbles
  • Use light traps @ 1/ha
  • Clip seedling tips before transplanting
Chemical
  • Cartap hydrochloride 4G @ 10kg/ha
  • Chlorantraniliprole 0.4G @ 10kg/ha
  • Spray Chlorpyriphos 20EC @ 1.25L/ha
Biological
  • Release Trichogramma japonicum @ 1 lakh/ha
  • Conserve spiders, dragonflies
Economic Threshold

5% dead hearts OR 1 egg mass/m²

Blast Disease

Fungal Disease
High Damage
Symptoms
  • Spindle-shaped spots with gray center
  • Node infection causes neck blast
  • White to grayish lesions on leaves
  • Complete yield loss in severe cases
Control Measures
Preventive
  • Use resistant varieties (IR-64, Swarna)
  • Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer
  • Maintain proper plant spacing
Chemical
  • Tricyclazole 75WP @ 0.6g/L water
  • Isoprothiolane 40EC @ 1.5ml/L
  • Carbendazim 50WP @ 1g/L
Cultural
  • Drain water for 3-4 days
  • Remove infected plant debris

Brown Plant Hopper

Insect Pest
Moderate Damage
Symptoms
  • Hopper burn - drying of plants
  • Sooty mold development
  • Presence of nymphs/adults at base
  • Yellowing and wilting
Control Measures
Cultural
  • Avoid close planting
  • Drain water for 2-3 days
  • Use resistant varieties
Chemical
  • Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 100ml/ha
  • Buprofezin 25SC @ 1.25L/ha
  • Pymetrozine 50WG @ 300g/ha
Biological
  • Conserve spiders, mirid bugs
  • Release aquatic beetles
Economic Threshold

20-25 hoppers/hill at vegetative stage

Organic Pest Control Methods

Neem-based Preparations

Neem Oil Spray

Preparation: Mix 5ml neem oil + 2ml soap in 1L water

Application: Spray early morning or late evening

Target: Aphids, thrips, leaf miners, caterpillars

Chilli-Garlic Extract

Natural Repellent

Preparation: Grind 100g chillies + 100g garlic in 1L water, strain

Application: Dilute 1:10 with water and spray

Target: Sucking pests, beetles, borers

Cow Urine Solution

Growth Promoter & Pest Repellent

Preparation: Mix 5L cow urine in 100L water

Application: Foliar spray at 15-day intervals

Benefits: Improves immunity, repels insects
