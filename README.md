# KrishiMitra — AI Farmer Advisory System

KrishiMitra is an AI-powered farmer advisory platform developed as a solution for **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025**, focused on addressing real-world challenges faced by **farmers in Kerala**.  
The system aims to provide timely, accurate, and localized agricultural guidance using modern technologies.

---

## 🌾 Problem Statement

Farmers in Kerala face multiple challenges such as:
- Unpredictable weather conditions  
- Pest and disease outbreaks  
- Limited access to expert agricultural advice  
- Crop loss due to lack of timely information  
- Language and digital literacy barriers  

Most small and marginal farmers do not have easy access to agricultural experts or data-driven insights, which affects productivity and income.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

**KrishiMitra** acts as a digital companion for farmers by offering:
- AI-based crop advisory and recommendations  
- Weather-based alerts and farming suggestions  
- Pest and disease identification with preventive measures  
- Support for local crops commonly grown in Kerala  
- Information delivered in a simple and farmer-friendly manner  

The platform is designed to be accessible, reliable, and scalable.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

- 🌦️ **Weather-based Crop Advisory**
- 🐛 **Pest & Disease Detection**
- 🌱 **Crop-Specific Recommendations**
- 🗣️ **Multilingual Support (including Malayalam)**
- 📱 **Farmer-Friendly Interface**
- 🤖 **AI & Data-Driven Insights**

## 🎯 Target Users

- Small and marginal farmers in Kerala
