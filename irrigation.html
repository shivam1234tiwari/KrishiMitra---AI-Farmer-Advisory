Modern Irrigation Methods

Drip Irrigation

30-50% Water Saving 40-60% Energy Saving 20-40% Yield Increase

Water delivered directly to plant roots through network of pipes, valves, and emitters.

Best For:

  • Vegetables & Fruits
  • Orchards & Plantations
  • Row Crops
  • Greenhouses

Components:

  • Drip Lines/Tubes
  • Emitters/Drippers
  • Filters & Valves
  • Fertigation System

Government Subsidy:

Up to 55% for small & marginal farmers

Sprinkler Irrigation

25-40% Water Saving Suitable for Light Soils Large Area Coverage

Water sprayed into air through nozzles, falling like rain on crop surface.

Best For:

  • Wheat & Cereals
  • Pastures & Lawns
  • Light Soils
  • Undulating Land

Types:

  • Portable Sprinklers
  • Solid Set Systems
  • Center Pivot
  • Rain Guns

Government Subsidy:

Up to 50% for various systems

Crop-specific Irrigation Guide

Rice Irrigation Management

Water requirement: 1000-1500 mm per crop cycle

Critical Irrigation Stages

Nursery Stage 0-25 days

Water Requirement: Continuous saturation

Depth: 2-3 cm standing water

Frequency: Daily irrigation

Transplanting 25-30 days

Water Requirement: Critical for establishment

Depth: 2-3 cm standing water

Frequency: Maintain constant level

Tillering 30-50 days

Water Requirement: Moderate

Depth: Alternate wetting-drying

Frequency: 3-5 days interval

Allow field to dry for 2-3 days to promote tillering

Panicle Initiation 50-70 days

Water Requirement: Critical stage

Depth: 5-7 cm standing water

Frequency: Maintain water depth

Water stress at this stage reduces yield by 50-60%

Flowering 70-90 days

Water Requirement: Critical

Depth: 3-5 cm standing water

Frequency: Maintain moisture

Grain Filling 90-110 days

Water Requirement: Moderate

Depth: Gradual drying

Frequency: Stop irrigation 15 days before harvest
