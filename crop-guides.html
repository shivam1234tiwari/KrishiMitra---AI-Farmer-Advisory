Rice Cultivation Guide

Oryza sativa - India's staple food crop

Kharif & Rabi 120-150 days Water Intensive

Recommended Varieties

High Yielding Varieties

  • Pusa Basmati 1509: Early maturing (115 days), aromatic
  • IR-64: Medium duration, disease resistant
  • Samba Mahsuri: Fine grain, high market value
  • Swarna: Popular in Eastern India

Hybrid Varieties

  • DRRH 3: 130 days, yield 7-8 t/ha
  • PA 6444: Suitable for saline soils
  • Pro Agro 6444: Bold grain, high yield

Special Purpose

  • Jaya: Drought tolerant
  • CR Dhan 801: Submergence tolerant
  • DRR Dhan 44: Zinc rich

Land Preparation

1

Initial Plowing

Deep plowing (20-25 cm) immediately after harvest of previous crop. Use moldboard plow or disc plow.

2

Harrowing

2-3 harrowings at 10-day intervals to break clods and incorporate stubble.

3

Puddling

Puddle field with 5-10 cm standing water. Perfect puddling: soil should be soft to knee depth.

4

Leveling

Use wooden plank or laser leveler to ensure uniform water depth (2-3 cm variation max).

Nursery Management

Seed Rate

20-25 kg/ha for transplanting, 40-50 kg/ha for direct seeding

Seed Treatment

  • Soak seeds in water for 12 hours
  • Treat with Carbendazim 50WP @ 2g/kg seed
  • Or use Trichoderma viride @ 4g/kg seed
  • For zinc deficiency: ZnSO₄ @ 4g/kg seed

Nursery Bed Preparation

  • Size: 10m × 1.2m for 1 kg seed
  • Soil: Fine tilth, well decomposed FYM
  • Fertilizer: 10g N, 8g P₂O₅, 5g K₂O per m²
  • Water: Maintain moist conditions

Transplanting & Spacing

Ideal Age for Transplanting

18-25 days old seedlings (4-5 leaf stage)

Spacing Recommendations

Transplanting Tips

  • Transplant in evening to reduce shock
  • Maintain 2-3 cm water depth
  • Transplant 2-3 seedlings per hill
  • Plant at 2-3 cm depth

Water Management

Critical Stages for Irrigation

Nursery Continuous saturation
Transplanting 2-3 cm standing water
Tillering Alternate wetting-drying
Panicle Initiation 5-7 cm water depth
Flowering 3-5 cm water depth
Grain Filling Gradual drying

Water Saving Techniques

  • SRI Method: 30-40% water saving
  • Alternate Wetting & Drying: 15-30% saving
  • Drip Irrigation: 50-60% saving
  • Laser Leveling: 10-15% saving

Weed Management

Manual Weeding

  • First weeding: 20-25 days after transplanting
  • Second weeding: 40-45 DAT if needed
  • Use hand hoe or cono weeder

Chemical Control

Herbicide Dose Time of Application
Butachlor 50% EC 1.25 kg a.i./ha 3 DAT (sand mix)
Pendimethalin 30% EC 1.0 kg a.i./ha 3-4 DAT
Bispyribac 10% SC 25 g a.i./ha 15-20 DAT

Harvesting & Post-Harvest

Harvesting Time

  • Indicators: 80-85% grains straw colored, moisture 20-25%
  • Time: 30-35 days after flowering
  • Method: Manual harvesting with sickle or combine harvester

Threshing & Drying

  • Thresh within 2-3 days of harvesting
  • Dry to 14% moisture content
  • Sun drying: 2-3 days, turn every 2 hours
  • Mechanical drying: 40-45°C for 4-6 hours

Storage

  • Clean and fumigate storage area
  • Use airtight containers/bags
  • Treat with malathion 50EC @ 10ml/ton
  • Maintain < 14% moisture, < 30°C temperature
