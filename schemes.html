Major Central Government Schemes

Most Popular

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Active

Benefit: ₹6,000 per year

Direct income support in three equal installments

Eligibility Criteria
  • All landholding farmer families
  • Ownership of cultivable land
  • Excludes institutional landholders
  • Excludes income tax payers
Application Process
  1. Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
  2. Submit land records and Aadhaar
  3. Fill PM-KISAN registration form
  4. Get registration ID for tracking
Payment Schedule
April-July ₹2,000
August-November ₹2,000
December-March ₹2,000

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Active

Premium: 1.5-5% of sum insured

Comprehensive crop insurance against natural calamities

Coverage
  • Yield loss due to natural calamities
  • Prevented sowing/planting risk
  • Post-harvest losses
  • Localized calamities
Premium Rates
Crop Season Food & Oilseed Crops Annual Commercial/Horticulture
Kharif 2% of sum insured 5% of sum insured
Rabi 1.5% of sum insured 5% of sum insured
