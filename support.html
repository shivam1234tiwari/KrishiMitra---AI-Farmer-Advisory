Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get a response?

For emergency helpline: Immediate response

For general queries: Within 24 hours

For field visits: Within 48 hours (depending on location)

Is the service free for farmers?

Yes, all KrishiMitra services are completely free for registered farmers.

Government scheme assistance and basic consultations are always free.

Can I get support in my local language?

Yes, we provide support in 10+ Indian languages including Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Odia.

What information should I provide for crop issues?

For best assistance, provide:

  • Crop name and variety
  • Growth stage of crop
  • Clear photos of affected plants
  • Location and soil type
  • Recent weather conditions

How do I track my government scheme application?

You can track your application through:

  • KrishiMitra app/website using your application ID
  • SMS updates (if registered)
  • Calling our helpline with application details
  • Visiting the concerned Krishi Bhavan
